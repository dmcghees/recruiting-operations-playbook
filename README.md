# Recruiting Operations Playbook

## Overview

This repository is a practical recruiting operations portfolio focused on the systems, coordination, communication, and documentation that support an organized hiring process.

It demonstrates how I approach recruiting workflows, interview coordination, candidate experience, hiring manager support, offer coordination, onboarding handoff, reporting, and process improvement.

The repository is designed around recruiting operations and coordination rather than full cycle recruiting.

## Areas Covered

This playbook will include:

* Recruiting intake and requisition support
* Candidate pipeline coordination
* Interview scheduling
* Candidate communication
* Hiring manager coordination
* Candidate experience
* Offer process support
* Recruiting to onboarding handoff
* Recruiting metrics and reporting
* Process documentation
* Recruiting workflow improvement

## Recruiting Operations Approach

My approach to recruiting operations focuses on organization, communication, consistency, candidate experience, and clear ownership.

A typical process includes:

1. Confirm the hiring need and required information.
2. Establish ownership and key stakeholders.
3. Track candidates through each stage.
4. Coordinate interviews and logistics.
5. Communicate clearly with candidates and interview teams.
6. Track feedback and outstanding actions.
7. Support offer and preboarding coordination.
8. Maintain accurate documentation.
9. Identify process gaps and opportunities for improvement.
