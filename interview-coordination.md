# Interview Coordination

## Purpose

Interview coordination is the operational backbone of a smooth hiring process.

This guide outlines a practical approach to scheduling interviews, managing interviewer availability, communicating with candidates, preparing interview teams, and tracking feedback.

The goal is to create a process that is organized, responsive, and easy for both candidates and internal teams to navigate.

## Core Responsibilities

Interview coordination may include:

* Candidate scheduling
* Interviewer calendar management
* Panel coordination
* Video meeting setup
* Candidate communication
* Interview confirmations
* Rescheduling
* Feedback tracking
* Hiring manager follow up
* Candidate experience support

## 1. Confirm the Interview Stage

Before scheduling, confirm:

* Candidate name
* Role
* Interview stage
* Interviewers
* Interview length
* Interview format
* Time zone
* Required preparation
* Scheduling deadline

Make sure the interview structure matches the approved hiring process.

## 2. Review Interviewer Availability

Check availability across all required interviewers.

When possible:

* Use shared scheduling blocks
* Avoid unnecessary calendar fragmentation
* Protect candidate wait time
* Account for interviewer time zones
* Identify backup interviewers
* Avoid scheduling interviews too tightly around major meetings

If several interviewers are required, confirm the full panel before contacting the candidate with a final schedule.

## 3. Candidate Availability

Ask the candidate for several available time options when appropriate.

Useful information may include:

* Preferred dates
* Available time ranges
* Time zone
* Scheduling constraints
* Accessibility or accommodation needs

Avoid asking candidates to provide more availability than necessary.

## 4. Build the Interview Schedule

For multi stage or panel interviews, create a clear schedule.

Example:

| Time | Interviewer | Focus | Length |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 10:00 AM | Hiring Manager | Role Experience | 45 min |
| 10:45 AM | Team Member | Collaboration | 30 min |
| 11:15 AM | Break | Candidate | 15 min |
| 11:30 AM | Department Lead | Final Discussion | 30 min |

Build in breaks for longer interview loops.

## 5. Send Candidate Confirmation

The candidate confirmation should include:

* Date
* Start time
* Time zone
* Interview format
* Interviewer names
* Meeting link or location
* Expected duration
* Preparation guidance
* Contact information for scheduling issues

Keep the message clear and easy to scan.

## 6. Prepare Interviewers

Interviewers should receive:

* Candidate name
* Role
* Interview time
* Resume or approved candidate materials
* Evaluation focus
* Interview guide when applicable
* Feedback deadline

Do not rely on interviewers to search through old emails for basic information.

## 7. Video Interview Setup

For virtual interviews:

* Create the approved video meeting
* Confirm the meeting link works
* Check time zone accuracy
* Avoid duplicate meeting links
* Confirm interviewers have access
* Include a backup contact method

For important or executive interviews, verify the meeting setup before the interview.

## 8. Onsite Interview Coordination

For onsite interviews, confirm:

* Building address
* Arrival instructions
* Reception contact
* Visitor registration
* Interview room
* Interviewer schedule
* Badge or access requirements
* Candidate parking or transportation information
* Accessibility needs

Notify reception or security when required.

## 9. Candidate Experience

Interview coordination affects how candidates perceive the organization.

Strong candidate experience includes:

* Prompt communication
* Clear instructions
* Respect for the candidate's time
* Limited unnecessary rescheduling
* Accurate interviewer information
* Reasonable breaks
* Timely updates

Even when a hiring decision is uncertain, scheduling communication should remain organized.

## 10. Rescheduling

When an interview must be moved:

1. Notify the candidate quickly.
2. Explain the change briefly.
3. Offer new options.
4. Update all interviewer calendars.
5. Update video links or rooms.
6. Confirm the new schedule.

Avoid making the candidate coordinate directly with multiple interviewers.

## 11. Last Minute Interviewer Cancellation

If an interviewer becomes unavailable:

* Identify the approved backup
* Confirm whether the interview can continue
* Notify the hiring manager
* Update the candidate only when necessary
* Avoid changing the candidate's schedule if an internal substitution can solve the issue

Document recurring interviewer availability problems if they repeatedly affect candidates.

## 12. Feedback Tracking

After the interview:

* Confirm feedback was submitted
* Follow up on missing feedback
* Track the decision status
* Notify the appropriate recruiting owner
* Avoid allowing feedback delays to stall the candidate indefinitely

Example tracker:

| Candidate | Stage | Interview Date | Feedback Due | Feedback Status | Next Step |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Candidate A | Hiring Manager | Oct 5 | Oct 6 | Complete | Team Interview |
| Candidate B | Team Interview | Oct 5 | Oct 6 | Pending | Follow Up |
| Candidate C | Final | Oct 6 | Oct 7 | Complete | Decision |

## 13. Interview Scheduling Metrics

Useful metrics may include:

* Average time to schedule interview
* Number of reschedules
* Candidate scheduling response time
* Interviewer cancellation rate
* Feedback completion time
* Interview no show rate

Metrics should be used to improve the process rather than create unnecessary reporting.

## 14. Common Scheduling Risks

Potential issues include:

* Interviewers with limited availability
* Candidates in different time zones
* Too many interview stages
* Last minute panel changes
* Missing meeting links
* Delayed interviewer feedback
* Calendar conflicts
* Unclear interview ownership

Identifying recurring problems can lead to better scheduling systems.

## Sample Interview Coordination Checklist

- [ ] Interview stage confirmed
- [ ] Interviewers confirmed
- [ ] Candidate availability received
- [ ] Time zones verified
- [ ] Calendar invitations sent
- [ ] Video link or room confirmed
- [ ] Candidate confirmation sent
- [ ] Interviewers received materials
- [ ] Reception or security notified if needed
- [ ] Backup interviewer identified
- [ ] Feedback deadline communicated
- [ ] Feedback tracked after interview

## Example Scenario

**Situation:** A candidate was scheduled for a three person interview panel, but one interviewer became unavailable the morning of the interview.

**Action taken:** Confirmed an approved backup interviewer, updated the calendar invitation, provided the backup interviewer with the interview focus and candidate materials, and kept the candidate's original schedule unchanged.

**Result:** The interview proceeded as planned without requiring the candidate to reschedule.

## Key Takeaway

Strong interview coordination removes unnecessary friction from the hiring process.

Clear ownership, accurate scheduling, thoughtful candidate communication, and consistent feedback tracking help recruiting teams move efficiently while creating a professional candidate experience.
