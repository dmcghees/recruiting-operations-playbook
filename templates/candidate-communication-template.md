# Candidate Communication Templates

## Purpose

This template provides examples of clear, professional candidate communication throughout the recruiting process.

Messages should be adapted to the role, organization, candidate, and actual hiring process.

## Interview Availability Request

**Subject:** Interview Availability for [Role]

Hi [Candidate Name],

Thank you for your continued interest in the [Role] position.

We would like to schedule the next step in the interview process. Please send a few times that work well for you during [date range], along with your time zone.

The conversation will be approximately [length] and will take place [virtually / onsite].

Once I receive your availability, I will confirm the schedule and send the full details.

Best,

[Name]

## Communication Guidelines

Candidate messages should generally be:

* Clear
* Brief
* Accurate
* Respectful
* Timely
* Free of unnecessary internal details

Avoid:

* Promising outcomes that are not approved
* Sharing confidential interviewer feedback
* Guessing about decision timelines
* Providing unapproved compensation information
* Leaving candidates without updates when significant delays occur
