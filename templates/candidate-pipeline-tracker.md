# Candidate Pipeline Tracker

## Purpose

This template provides a centralized view of candidates moving through the recruiting process.

The goal is to make candidate status, next actions, ownership, deadlines, and delays visible.

## Pipeline Stages

### New Application

Candidate has entered the system but has not been reviewed.

### Recruiter Review

Candidate qualifications are under initial review.

### Recruiter Screen

Initial recruiting conversation is scheduled or complete.

### Hiring Manager Review

Candidate has been submitted to the hiring manager.

### Interviewing

Candidate is actively completing interviews.

### Final Review

Interview process is complete and the hiring decision is pending.

### Offer

Offer preparation, approval, or candidate review is underway.

### Hired

Candidate accepted the offer.

### Closed

Candidate is no longer active.

## Pipeline Risks

Potential risks include:

* Candidate waiting too long for feedback
* Hiring manager review overdue
* Interviewer availability
* Offer approval delayed
* Candidate considering another offer
* Candidate communication overdue
* Process stage changed unexpectedly

## Weekly Pipeline Review

* [ ] Review candidates with no recent activity

* [ ] Assign owners to outstanding actions

## Key Takeaway

A useful candidate pipeline tracker should always make it easy to answer:

**Where is the candidate?**

**How long have they been there?**

**What happens next?**

**Who owns the next step?**
