# Interview Plan Template

## Purpose

This template helps hiring teams define the structure, purpose, and ownership of an interview process before candidates begin interviewing.

The goal is to reduce repetitive interviews, clarify evaluation areas, and create a more consistent candidate experience.

## Interview Objectives

### Recruiter Screen

Evaluate:

* Candidate interest
* Relevant background
* Work arrangement alignment
* Compensation alignment when appropriate
* Basic role expectations
* Candidate questions

### Hiring Manager Interview

Evaluate:

* Relevant experience
* Scope of previous responsibilities
* Role specific skills
* Problem solving
* Career goals
* Team needs

### Team Interview

Evaluate:

* Collaboration
* Communication
* Functional skills
* Cross functional experience
* Situational problem solving

### Final Interview

Evaluate:

* Remaining decision areas
* Leadership alignment
* Final candidate questions
* Overall hiring recommendation
