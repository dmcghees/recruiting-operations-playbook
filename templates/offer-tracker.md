# Offer Tracker

## Purpose

This template provides a centralized view of candidates moving through the offer process.

The goal is to track approvals, offer preparation, candidate response, start dates, and recruiting to onboarding handoff while maintaining clear ownership and confidentiality.

## Offer Status Definitions

### Preparing

Offer details are being confirmed or documentation is being prepared.

### Awaiting Approval

One or more required approvals remain outstanding.

### Approved

All required internal approvals are complete.

### Extended

The offer has been formally presented to the candidate.

### Candidate Reviewing

The candidate is considering the offer.

### Accepted

The candidate has formally accepted.

### Declined

The candidate declined the offer.

### Withdrawn

The offer process ended for another approved reason.

* Another offer
* Work arrangement
* Location
* Timing
* Role scope
* Personal decision

Do not infer a reason the candidate did not provide.

## Offer Metrics

Potential metrics include:

* Number of offers extended
* Offer acceptance rate
* Offer approval time
* Time from final interview to offer
* Candidate response time
* Offer decline reasons
* Time from acceptance to onboarding handoff

## Confidentiality

Offer information may contain sensitive compensation and employment information.

Use only approved systems for storing:

* Compensation details
* Offer documents
* Candidate personal information
* Approval records

Portfolio versions of this tracker should use fictional or placeholder candidate information.

## Key Takeaway

Offer coordination depends on accurate information, clear approvals, confidentiality, and timely follow up.

A centralized tracker makes it easier to see what is blocking an offer and what needs to happen next.
