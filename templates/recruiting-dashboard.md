# Recruiting Operations Dashboard

## Purpose

This dashboard provides a high level view of recruiting operations across open requisitions, candidate pipelines, interview coordination, feedback, offers, upcoming hires, and process risks.

The goal is to help Recruiting Operations identify bottlenecks and priorities without reviewing every candidate record individually.

## Status Definitions

### On Track

Work is progressing within expected timelines.

### Attention Needed

Follow up is required, but the issue is not yet significantly affecting hiring.

### At Risk

A delay or dependency may affect candidates or hiring timelines.

### Critical

Immediate action is required because the hiring process is blocked or a significant candidate experience issue exists.

## Candidate Experience Risks

Potential risks may include:

* Excessive rescheduling
* Candidate waiting without an update
* Interview process longer than expected
* Interviewer no show
* Inconsistent interview instructions
* Delayed final decision
* Delayed offer approval

## Operational Risks

| Risk | Impact | Owner | Mitigation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Limited interviewer availability | Scheduling delays | Hiring Manager | Reserve interview blocks |
| Overdue feedback | Candidate delay | Recruiting | Daily follow up |
| Approval bottleneck | Offer delay | HR / Finance | Escalate aging approvals |

## Key Takeaway

A recruiting operations dashboard turns individual hiring activities into one clear view of the recruiting system.

It helps identify bottlenecks earlier, maintain candidate momentum, and give recruiting and hiring leaders visibility into where operational support is needed.
