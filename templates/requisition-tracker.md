# Recruiting Requisition Tracker

## Purpose

This template provides a centralized view of open hiring requests.

The goal is to track ownership, hiring status, recruiting activity, deadlines, risks, and upcoming actions across multiple roles.

## Status Definitions

### Open

The requisition is actively hiring.

### On Hold

Recruiting activity has temporarily stopped.

### Offer

A candidate is in the offer process.

### Filled

A candidate has accepted the role.

### Closed

The requisition is no longer active.
